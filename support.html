Frequently Asked Questions

When will my deposit be approved?

Deposits are usually confirmed within a few hours during working hours. Make sure you transferred the exact amount to 9033527049 (OPay).

When can I withdraw?

Main balance: only from the 30th to the 6th of every month. Referral earnings: daily between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

How do referral earnings work?

You earn ₦200 only when your friend signs up and successfully deposits/upgrades their plan.

Why is my plan still Free after payment?

Your payment is pending admin confirmation. Once approved, your plan will upgrade automatically.
